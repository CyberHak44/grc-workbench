Heat map (current risk)

Rows: likelihood L1 (rare) to L5 (almost certain). Columns: impact I1 (minor) to I5 (severe). Hover a cell to see its risks.

Score = likelihood x impact, 1 to 25. Risks scoring at or above the appetite line need a treatment plan and an owner's sign-off.

IDRiskAssetsLIInherentResidualTreatmentOwnerStatus

Treatment options follow ISO/IEC 27001 clause 6.1.3: treat (add controls), tolerate (accept within appetite), transfer (insurance or contract), terminate (stop the activity). Controls are linked to risks in the SoA tab.

CBUAE Operational Risk Management readiness

The 39 cyber, resilience, third-party and notification requirements of CBUAE Circular C 1/2026. Each starts with an automatic rating built from your SoA and suppliers; you confirm or override it with evidence. Readiness = (Met + half of Partial) / assessed.

Suppliers and third-party risk

Tier sets how hard a supplier is checked. Tier 1: supports a Critical Operation, holds restricted data, or has admin or remote access. Tier 2: confidential data or read access. Tier 3: everything else. Evidence is scored on what the supplier proved, not what it claimed: evidenced 2, claimed 1, missing 0.

IDSupplierTierEvidenceOutcomeAssetsFlags

Outcomes: accept (evidence meets the tier's bar), remediate (close named gaps by a date), compensate (add your own controls around the supplier), exit (plan to replace). Linked to A.5.19 to A.5.22 and CBUAE Article 13.

Statement of Applicability

ISO/IEC 27001 clause 6.1.3 d: list every Annex A control, say whether it applies, justify both inclusion and exclusion, and record whether it is implemented. Link each control to the risks it treats. An auditor reads this document first.
