Criticality: 1 minor, 5 the business stops. Owner is a role, not a person, so the register survives staff changes.

Heat map (current risk)

Rows: likelihood L1 (rare) to L5 (almost certain). Columns: impact I1 (minor) to I5 (severe). Hover a cell to see its risks.

Score = likelihood x impact, 1 to 25. Risks scoring at or above the appetite line need a treatment plan and an owner's sign-off.

IDRiskAssetsLIInherentResidualTreatmentOwnerStatus

Treatment options follow ISO/IEC 27001 clause 6.1.3: treat (add controls), tolerate (accept within appetite), transfer (insurance or contract), terminate (stop the activity). Controls are linked to risks in the SoA tab.

Statement of Applicability

ISO/IEC 27001 clause 6.1.3 d: list every Annex A control, say whether it applies, justify both inclusion and exclusion, and record whether it is implemented. Link each control to the risks it treats. An auditor reads this document first.
